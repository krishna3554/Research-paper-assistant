The Transformer architecture was introduced in the paper "Attention Is All You Need" by Vaswani et al. in 2017.

The main idea of the Transformer is to replace recurrence with self-attention. Instead of processing tokens one after another like an RNN, the Transformer allows every token to attend to every other token in the sequence.

The original Transformer uses multi-head attention. Multi-head attention lets the model learn different relationships between words at the same time. In the base Transformer model, the authors used 8 attention heads.

The Transformer also uses positional encoding because self-attention alone does not know the order of tokens. Positional encodings are added to token embeddings so the model can use sequence order.

The encoder is made of stacked self-attention and feed-forward layers. The decoder uses masked self-attention, encoder-decoder attention, and feed-forward layers.

The Transformer achieved strong results on machine translation tasks and became the foundation for many later language models.
